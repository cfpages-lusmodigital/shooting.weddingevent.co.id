- Geographic repetition, archives, metadata, and generic quality claims are excluded.

## Position and boundary

The site should help couples, families, companies, planners, and creative teams brief, produce, approve, deliver, and preserve event photography/video. It does not promise artistic or commercial outcomes, provide drone/venue/rights approval, or reuse personal media without consent.

## Evidence gates

- Crew size, camera/audio/light equipment, coverage, redundancy, turnaround, storage, backup, resolution, colour, and deliverable claims require a dated written scope and tested workflow.
- Venue access, drone use, music, performance, brand, talent, minors, guests, private rituals, and public recording require applicable permission, release, and current rules.
- Personal images, audio, guest lists, and event data require consent, access control, retention/deletion decisions, and secure transfer.
- Prices, schedules, revisions, copyright, usage licence, raw-file access, and archive period require written terms. Sample work requires client/subject permission and context.
